## Decisions

| Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- |
| Own STEP writer instead of OpenCascade in the browser | Writes the exact loft surfaces as text; no WebAssembly download of several MB; validated in CI with OpenCascade. |
| Upper and lower surface as separate STEP faces, split at the leading edge by knot insertion | Avoids a seam edge on a periodic surface; every edge is an exact boundary iso-curve. |
| Sections resampled at common cosine-spaced chord fractions | Point j of every section corresponds by chord position, so blending and lofting keep the leading edge at one parameter. |
| Section origin at the NURBS leading edge (minimum x of the curve), chord to the trailing-edge midpoint | Placement refers to the interpolated shape, not to the nearest file point. |
| Inclined airfoils are not derotated | Some tables (e.g. Göttingen 795) use a baseline instead of the leading-edge-to-trailing-edge chord; twist refers to the file's x axis. A warning reports the inclination. |
| Spanwise C0 knots at sections in linear mode | Straight panels with kinks at sections stay exact; smooth mode uses one C2 spline. |
| Split parameters within 1e-10 of an existing knot snap to that knot | A symmetric airfoil puts the leading edge 1 ulp (unit in the last place) from a knot; inserting a separate knot there gives multiplicity 5 and an invalid STEP face. |
| Smooth spanwise blending reports negative blended thickness as an error | The cubic cardinal functions overshoot between sections with large chord or thickness changes; the upper surface then passes below the lower one. |
| Smooth mode: a blended value more than 2 section ranges outside the section value range is an error (`OVERSHOOT_LIMIT`) | Sections 0.1 mm apart next to 100 mm gaps give cardinal weights of ±1475: 750 random smooth wings (NACA sections, 5 seeds) produced surfaces with inverted thickness down to -253 m and deviations of 12.8 m that every other check passed; with the limit, 0 of 750 builds without error have a negative thickness on a 4000 x 59 sample grid. Limit 2, not 1: a curved planform with chords 100/500/100 mm at y = 0/100/1000 mm reaches 1.34 ranges; tip clusters (20 to 200 mm chords at y = 0/500/510 mm) reach 6.6. 648 preset builds (6 presets x flat/pointed x linear/smooth x 3 chord samples x 3 parametrizations x 3 trailing-edge modes): no error. |
| Fitted surface probed at 257 span positions, guide breakpoints and the quarter points of every station interval: chord, local thickness and contact are errors; the deviation warning and added stations use the check positions only | Global cubic interpolation through closely spaced stations can swing between them while every fixed check position lies near a station; zigzag degree-5 guides make the fitted surface fail between stations in 4 of 40 random cases (trials 2, 18 and 23 turn inside out, trial 26 folds), and the 36 accepted ones pass a dense check (401 rows without crossing, no negative thickness on 2001 span samples; 40 seeded trials, sample wing, degree-5 control guides). Within 2 mm of a pointed elliptic tip the loft deviated 0.3 to 0.8 mm between stations with the earlier guide ends (more than 10 % of the 1 to 3 mm chord there; with the 11-point guides of the current wizard the edges deviate at most 0.031 mm there); with the quarter points driving added stations, 4 of 6 pointed wizard presets still warned after 13 to 21 added stations. |
| Guide curves stretched to root-to-tip span | Guides follow span edits without manual correction. |
| Section planes: **Mitred** as default, **Vertical** as the other choice; version 1 files open vertical (owner decisions R1 and R2 of 2026-09-30, `docs/Flow5upgrade.md`) | Vertical planes make a panel with dihedral δ cos δ as thick as its airfoil (81.9 % on a 35° V-tail); XFLR5, flow5 and OpenVSP mitre. A version 1 file keeps the shape it was designed with; an older app refuses version 2 instead of dropping the setting. |
| Mitred planes from the section positions (y, z), with an optional stored panel angle per section (`panelAngle`, owner decision "Store XFLR5's angles" of 2026-09-30); the XFLR5 import stores XFLR5's dihedral where airfoil frames turn a panel by more than 0.001° | An airfoil frame moves the two sections of a panel differently, so the dihedral of the moved sections differs from XFLR5's (untilted Fixture A: 2.92° and 4.88° instead of 3° and 6°). Storing the roll alone changes nothing: on 284 random untilted parts with cambered NACA sections the largest distance of a built section from XFLR5's placement is 0.430 mm with XFLR5's roll and the stretch of the moved sections, 0.417 mm without stored angles; the worst case sits at a vertical root, all stretch (both measured with frame offsets up to 1e-3 of the chord dropped). The panel angle gives roll and stretch; with exact airfoil frames (owner decision "Always exact" below) the largest distance is 0.033 mm, the sag of the reference polyline between the generated points: the built sections lie within 0.002 mm of the exact NACA curves placed as XFLR5 places them (2026-09-30). A flat wing whose tip airfoil lies 10 % of the chord off (0, 0): 0.017 mm (0.80 mm before). The angle stays when a section moves, as offered; a section inserted halfway along a panel copies it. |
| A panel less than 1 mm wide in y counts as no panel; its sections share the bisector plane of the panels around it (owner decision "Bisector for short panels" of 2026-09-30) | XFLR5 skips panels shorter than 0.1 mm and puts their sections in one plane. The import moves one of two sections at one y 0.5 mm apart; their own planes folded, and the part fell back to vertical planes. The width in y, not the length: the airfoil frames move such sections by up to a few mm in their shared plane, mostly in z. Current limitation: a panel 0.1 to 1 mm long in an XFLR5 file is a panel in XFLR5 and none in Wingdesigner. |
| **Linear** panels between planes of different roll get the stations per panel (R4) | The airfoil lies in every plane between, as with guide curves. With one station per panel the loft would be the ruled surface of **Straight panels**. Presets with dihedral build 9 or 17 stations; the Sport wing follows the **Linear** blend exactly (1.4 % less volume than the ruled vertical build, 0.343 mm off it). Current limitation: any roll difference switches the rule; a dihedral of 1e-6° instead of 0° moves the surface up to 0.17 mm (NACA 2412 at 300 mm to NACA 0009 at 100 mm, −4° twist, 600 mm panel; volume 0.25 %). |
| Stretch limit 2 (60°) and fold tests are build errors; the stretch is never clamped (R6) | 1/cos grows without bound; a clamped stretch would build the wrong thickness without a message. The fold test runs between neighbouring sections of **Straight panels** before the loft (`planeFold`), at every check position of a **Linear** panel whose planes turn (the rate cos φ + tan δ · sin φ − t · dφ/dy across the plane of the station; at a section for both panels next to it), and between neighbouring stations after the fit. The end-plane test covers **Straight panels** only: on **Linear** panels it stops surfaces that do not fold (NACA 0021 at 400 and 100 mm chord, a flat 33 mm panel before an 80° panel: turning margin 0.116), while the ruled surface of **Straight panels** folds there. A plane that rounds to 60.0° builds (limit 60.05°): section positions hold 4 decimals. The code review of 4534c32 found that the station test alone missed a fold inside the first station interval: 3 stations per panel built, and the right half crossed y = 0 by 0.08 to 0.7 mm; 4 and more stations reported it. |
