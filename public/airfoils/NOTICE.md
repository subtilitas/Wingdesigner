# Airfoil data notice

The files in this directory are airfoil coordinate data. They are not part of the Wingdesigner program code, and the MIT License (named after the Massachusetts Institute of Technology) of the program code does not apply to them. Each file keeps the terms of its source. This notice gives the source, the legal basis, the conditions and the attribution of every file. `index.json` repeats the author, the license identifier, the source address and the terms address of each file.

Five files are transcriptions of tables in reports of the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA), a United States Government agency. They are public domain in the United States; their status outside the United States is not established. One file, S9104, is published by its designer under an open license. 56 files are MH airfoils of Martin Hepperle, included with his written permission ([MH airfoils](#mh-airfoils)).

Whether a bare list of airfoil coordinates is protected by copyright or by a database right is not settled in every country. The statements below give the basis on which each file is distributed. They do not decide that question. This notice is not legal advice.

No file in this directory is a copy of a file from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC) Airfoil Coordinates Database.

### Reference line

The Clark Y and USA 35B tables give the ordinates from a straight base line under the lower surface, not from the chord line (the line from the leading edge to the trailing edge). The files keep the published values, so the leading edge is not at y = 0.

| Airfoil | Leading edge above the base line | Trailing edge above the base line (upper / lower surface) | Inclination of the chord line to the x axis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clark Y | 3.50 % of chord | 0.12 % / 0.00 % of chord | 1.97°, leading edge up |
| USA 35B | 2.76 % of chord | 0.25 % / 0.00 % of chord | 1.51°, leading edge up |

- Clark Y: the lower surface lies on the base line from 30 % of chord to the trailing edge.
- USA 35B: the lower surface touches the base line at 15 % of chord and at the trailing edge.
- The Wingdesigner import reports the inclination as the warning "rotated" and keeps the coordinates. Twist refers to the x axis of the file. At the same twist setting, the chord line of Clark Y is 1.97° and the chord line of USA 35B is 1.51° more nose-up than the chord line of a section whose chord lies on the x axis.
- NACA M-6 and NACA 8-H-12 use the chord line: their leading edge is at (0, 0).

### Transcription

The file is a transcription by Wingdesigner contributors of Reference No. 639 in the page image of the NTRS scan, in the same form as the NACA tables above: published ordinates in percent of chord divided by 100, 4 decimals, 33 points (17 stations per surface, leading-edge point (0, 0) listed once), closed trailing edge. ### Uncertain values

The scan of the table is a 1-bit image at about 300 dots per inch. In 7 cells one digit cannot be read with certainty. The file uses the more likely reading, chosen by comparison with digits of certain identity from the same page and typewriter and by the smoothness of the surface. No second, independent scan of the table is accessible to settle these cells: the archive.org copies are the NTRS files, and the HathiTrust image server refuses access.

| Surface | x (% of chord) | Value in the file (% of chord) | y in `raf-34.dat` | Other possible reading (% of chord) | Possible difference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upper | 1.25 | 1.96 | 0.0196 | 1.98 | 0.02 % of chord |
| Upper | 40 | 8.08 | 0.0808 | 8.06 | 0.02 % of chord |
| Upper | 90 | 1.26 | 0.0126 | 1.28 | 0.02 % of chord |
| Lower | 20 | -4.16 | -0.0416 | -4.18 | 0.02 % of chord |
| Lower | 60 | -3.69 | -0.0369 | -3.89 | 0.20 % of chord |
| Lower | 70 | -3.09 | -0.0309 | -3.08 | 0.01 % of chord |
| Lower | 95 | -0.76 | -0.0076 | -0.78 | 0.02 % of chord |

- Largest possible difference: 0.20 % of chord, at the lower surface at 60 % of chord. At a chord of 200 mm this is 0.40 mm; 0.02 % of chord is 0.04 mm.
- Lower surface at 60 % of chord: the reading -3.89 % of chord puts a kink in the lower surface; -3.69 % of chord gives an even change of slope over the neighbouring stations.
- In 6 further cells the digit shape alone is weak, but one reading remains. Upper surface 2.5 % (2.82, not 2.62), 10 % (5.83, not 5.63), 60 % (5.87, not 5.67) and 95 % of chord (0.64, not 0.84), lower surface 5 % of chord (-2.81, not -2.61): the other reading, 0.20 % of chord away, breaks the trend of the surface slope. Lower surface 7.5 % of chord (-3.25, not -3.26): the last digit matches the certain 5s of the page better than the 6s. These cells are not counted as uncertain.
